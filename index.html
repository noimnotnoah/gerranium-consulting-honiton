About Gerranium

Practical guidance for ambitious businesses

Based in the heart of East Devon, Gerranium is a business consultancy that believes in the power of good ideas. We work with business owners and leadership teams to clarify thinking, challenge assumptions, and turn aspirations into action — without imposing a one-size-fits-all prescription.

Whether you're starting out, growing, or pivoting, we bring a fresh perspective grounded in real commercial experience. Our motto says it all: we won't tell you how to run your business — we'll just help you with your ideas.
